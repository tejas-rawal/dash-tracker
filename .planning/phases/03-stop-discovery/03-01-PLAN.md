Riders (via future client apps) can retrieve the ordered, per-direction list of stops served by a route, by hitting `GET /api/v1/routes/:shortName/stops`. Purpose: Satisfy requirement STOP-01 and roadmap Phase 3 success criteria #1 and #3 (grouped, ordered stops for a valid route; typed 404 for an unknown route).
Output: `StopDiscovery.ts` response type, `StopService.ts`, `StopController.ts`, a new nested route on `busRoutes.ts`, and full unit + integration test coverage.
</objective>
