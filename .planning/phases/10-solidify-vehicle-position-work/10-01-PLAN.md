Purpose: Closes the "unverified against a live payload" risk flagged in `STATE.md`'s Blockers/Concerns (Quick 260915-fc8 entry) and `ROADMAP.md`'s Phase 10 context — field shapes are now confirmed against a real SFMTA payload captured during `/gsd-discuss-phase`, and route filtering now validates against known routes instead of forwarding arbitrary strings to the upstream API.
Output: `GET /api/v1/vehicles` returns correctly-mapped, 404-validated, coordinate-safe vehicle positions; `VehicleService` takes `BusDataRepository` via factory DI like every other service in the codebase.
