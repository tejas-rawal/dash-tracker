<objective>
Stop letting a DASH outage keep the server down. The server listens as soon as SQLite is open. Route/stop data loads in the background with capped exponential backoff and jitter until the first success, then refreshes hourly for the life of the process. Both background loops stop cleanly on SIGTERM/SIGINT.

Purpose:
- BOOT-01: listen even when DASH is down.
- BOOT-02: background retry with backoff.
- DATA-01: interval refresh.
- DATA-02: a failed refresh is logged and the last good data stays.
Together these close success criteria 1, 3 and 5 and finish SC2's "200 without a restart". The loader drives plan 13-03's single `load()` path.
Output:
- the new `src/server/api/services/BusDataRefreshService.ts` and its fake-timer tests;
- the D-18 boot order and shutdown in `src/server/app.ts`;
- `ServiceAlertPollService.stop()` (D-17);
- the real-app E2E `src/server/api/routes/dataBootRetry.test.ts`;
- a blackholed-DASH log assertion in `upstreamTimeout.test.ts`;
- a real-process smoke test of `app.ts`, which has no unit test and is excluded from coverage.

Claude's-discretion choices (CONTEXT):
- Jitter is **equal jitter**: half the capped ceiling plus a random half. Delays stay random but never shrink between consecutive attempts, so the logs visibly show "increasing delays" (SC1). Full jitter could log a shorter delay after a longer one.
- Tests control randomness with `vi.spyOn(Math, "random")`, and `computeRetryDelayMs` also takes an optional `random` argument for pure unit tests. No clock or scheduler dependency is injected; `vi.useFakeTimers()` covers scheduling.
- D-15 open point: `stop()` also prevents an attempt that is already in flight from rescheduling. A `stopped` flag is checked before any new timer is set, and `start()` after `stop()` is a no-op.

Resolved PATTERNS discrepancy 5 (D-08, one log line per attempt): the repository no longer logs (plan 13-03), and this loader writes the single warn or info line per attempt. The counts that used to be in the repository's "Processed N routes" line now come from `load()`'s summary.

Prohibition probe (spec-less fallback): one bespoke prohibition kept here (a failed load or refresh must never pass silently). This is the user's explicit wish that malformed-upstream refresh failures stay visible. A retry storm against DASH was recalled but dropped as routine engineering; truths 3 and 6 cover it.
</objective>
