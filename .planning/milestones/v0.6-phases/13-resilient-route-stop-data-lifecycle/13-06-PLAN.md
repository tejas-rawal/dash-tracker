<objective>
Close the one blocker in 13-VERIFICATION.md (gap truth 6, review WR-02).

The problem: `BusDataRepository.createStop` accepts a stop whose code, lat or lon is absent or null, because its checks only reject a literal NaN. `createRoute` and `createDirection` accept a missing id. So a malformed hourly refresh:
- silently replaces the last good dataset;
- clears lastError;
- is logged as a success.

That breaks D-12 (all-or-nothing loads), the 13-03 prohibition, and the user's explicit wish (13-CONTEXT specifics) that a malformed-upstream refresh be visible.

The fix:
- Validate those fields inside the existing single load path, so a malformed payload becomes an ordinary rejected `load()`.
- Riders keep the last good data.
- `lastFailedAt`/`lastError` record the failure (D-14).
- The 13-05 loader logs its one warn line.

The plan also refreshes the stale Architecture guidance in `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md` (review IN-05, which the verifier recommended doing after gap closure). Phase 14's planner and executors read that section as ground truth.

Purpose: DATA-02 (a failed refresh keeps the last good data) and DATA-03 (`lastLoadedAt` reflects only successful loads), extended to the malformed-field input class the original tests missed.

Output:
- validation in `src/server/api/repositories/BusDataRepository.ts`;
- a new E2E step in `src/server/api/routes/dataReload.test.ts`;
- the malformed-field matrix in `src/server/api/repositories/BusDataRepository.test.ts`;
- refreshed Architecture sections in `.claude/CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md`.

Claude's-discretion choice: use hand-rolled finite and id checks, not a Zod schema for the whole routes payload. The verifier accepts either. Reasons:
- **Scope.** Only five fields need validating (stop code/lat/lon and the three ids). A Zod schema forces a strictness decision on every payload field (type, names, headSigns, title).
- **Risk.** Under D-12 all-or-nothing loads that retry forever (D-06), any over-strict field turns an unanticipated live-payload variation into a permanent 503. That is the third prohibition.
- **Precedent.** Earlier upstream-payload guards use Number.isFinite (Phase 10 VehicleService, Phase 11 NearbyPredictionService).
- **Messages.** The existing operator-readable, id-bearing messages stay as they are; `lastError` carries them to Phase 14 readiness.

The choice is reversible: the validation is private to one file and sits behind the unchanged `load()` contract.

Not in this plan:
- WR-01 (stranded SSE streams after a refresh drops a stop) is plan 13-07, which runs in parallel.
- WR-03 (readiness signal) is deferred to Phase 14 per VERIFICATION.md.
- WR-04 is a follow-up and is not required.
- The deferred per-stop drop-and-warn idea stays out. One malformed stop still fails the whole load (D-12).
</objective>
