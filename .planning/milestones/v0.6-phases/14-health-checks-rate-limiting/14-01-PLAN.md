<objective>
Give a host or load balancer two probes:
- **Liveness**: is the process up?
- **Readiness**: is the route/stop data loaded and fresh enough to serve, and how old is it?

Both read only in-memory state and never call DASH.

Purpose:
- HLTH-01 (ROADMAP SC1): liveness is a constant 200 that never touches data state or DASH.
- HLTH-02 (SC2): readiness answers 200 when the data is loaded and fresher than the staleness threshold, and 503 when the data was never loaded or has gone stale. It reports the data age in both cases.

Output:
- `GET /api/v1/health/live` and `GET /api/v1/health/ready`, built routes → controller → service with factory DI (D-01).
- The staleness threshold `STALE_AFTER_MS = 3 * REFRESH_INTERVAL_MS + 5 * 60_000`, using the newly exported `REFRESH_INTERVAL_MS` (D-06, D-07).
- Unit tests for the service and controller, plus a real-app E2E.

Decisions honored:
- D-01: paths, mount and layering.
- D-02: liveness is a constant.
- D-03: readiness body shape.
- D-04: `lastError` is omitted.
- D-05: readiness gates on route/stop data only.
- D-06 / D-07 (revised): 3 intervals plus a 5-minute grace, held in a named constant.
- D-08: the refresh scheduler is untouched; the only edit is the export.
- D-19: both probes send `Cache-Control: no-store`; the readiness 503 sends no Retry-After.
- RESEARCH Pattern 3 is followed, but its comment and boundary values are replaced by the revised D-06. The boundary is now 11_100_000 ms, not 3 h.

Claude's-discretion choices (CONTEXT):
- `dataAgeSeconds` is floored to whole seconds and clamped at 0.
- The clock is controlled with `vi.useFakeTimers({ toFake: ["Date"] })` and not injected, so the factory keeps one argument like `createVehicleService(repository)`.
- `ReadinessReport` is service-local in `HealthService.ts` (the `NearbyPredictionService.ts` precedent), which keeps the tracer at five files.
- All readiness logic lives in the service. The controller only maps status codes and sets headers.

Spec-less probe fallback (no SPEC.md):
- Edges: all four HLTH edge items are resolved explicitly as truths (HLTH-01 concurrency; HLTH-02 boundary, precision and concurrency). None is left unresolved.
- Prohibitions:
  - Recall surfaced these candidates: probes leaking upstream error text or secrets; liveness turning into a dependency check; readiness flapping on one DASH blip; readiness triggering upstream load; probes being cached.
  - Kept: one privacy prohibition (no error text or secrets in probe bodies, D-04).
  - Dropped: "liveness becomes a dependency check" and "readiness triggers load" restate requirements already pinned as truths. "Flapping on one blip" is the HLTH-02 boundary truth. Caching is the D-19 header truth.
  - Canon: secret leakage in general is covered by /gsd-secure-phase; it is not minted again here.
</objective>
