## Prohibition Recall (FAV-01..05, DEVICE-01 — prohibition-probe Stage 1/2)

- **Kept** — #4 (silent cap on the "no cap" list), #8 (permissive entityType matching), #1 (shared-bucket fallback on missing device id). All three are genuine project-specific value decisions an engineer could plausibly reintroduce without the (nonexistent) SPEC forbidding it explicitly; none are routine-engineering bugs a generic code review would catch by default. Lifted into `must_haves.prohibitions` above.
- **Dropped as already-covered truths, not prohibitions** — #2, #3, #7 are the direct positive phrasing of FAV-01..04's idempotency/existence-check contract, already captured as `must_haves.truths` and enforced by Task 1/2's tests; restating their negation would be redundant.
- **Dropped as already-locked CONTEXT.md decisions** — #5 (hydrate via `BusDataRepository` directly, not `BusRouteService`/`StopService`) and #6 (no denormalized snapshot — hydrate live) are explicit, already-cited decisions in `06-CONTEXT.md`'s "List & Hydration" and "Repository Additions" sections; the tasks below implement them directly rather than re-minting them as a prohibition.
- **Dropped, redundant with SC #5** — #9 is already ROADMAP Phase 6 Success Criterion #5 ("all existing v0.2 endpoints continue to respond exactly as before"), already a `must_haves.truths` entry.
